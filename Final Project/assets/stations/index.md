# Station form exploration

All images are proposed designs, not built or tested hardware. The cutaways show conceptual component placement, not verified wiring or fabrication plans. Each image has its exact generation prompt alongside it.

- [Mill form 1](mill-form-1.png) — A hand circles a joystick on a low square cardboard box.
- [Mill form 2](mill-form-2.png) — A hand circles a joystick on a round cardboard drum.
- [Mill form 3](mill-form-3.png) — A hand circles a joystick on a tapered cardboard pedestal.
- [Mill cutaway](mill-cutaway.png) — A joystick board sits beneath the top with its Qwiic cable exiting the box.
- [Rolling form 1](rolling-form-1.png) — Hands roll a plain cardboard tube back and forth.
- [Rolling form 2](rolling-form-2.png) — Hands roll a tube with shaped end collars.
- [Rolling form 3](rolling-form-3.png) — Hands roll a short tube with narrow end bands.
- Rolling cutaway — **Missing:** image generation was repeatedly rejected by the image service; the proposed IMU and Qwiic placement remains unillustrated. Cable twist during rolling needs mechanical testing.
- [Oven form 1](oven-form-1.png) — A hand faces the arched mouth of a square cardboard oven.
- [Oven form 2](oven-form-2.png) — A hand faces the mouth of a broad domed cardboard oven.
- [Oven form 3](oven-form-3.png) — A hand faces the recessed rectangular mouth of a tapered oven.
- [Oven cutaway](oven-cutaway.png) — A proximity sensor faces the hand from inside the mouth, with its cable leading out of the housing.

The oven heat marks are symbolic; the concept senses hand distance and does not specify a physical heater. The rolling form alternatives are visually similar and their end treatments need physical prototyping to compare.
